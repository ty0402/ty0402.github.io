### MMEdit: A Unified Framework for Multi-Type Audio Editing via Audio Language Model (arXiv 2025)

**Ye Tao**, Xuenan Xu, Wen Wu, Shuai Wang, Mengyue Wu, Chao Zhang.

- Proposes a unified audio editing framework covering addition, replacement, removal, reordering, and attribute modification in one system.
- Combines a Qwen2-Audio-based multimodal encoder with an MMDiT generator to improve instruction following and edit localization while preserving non-edited content.
- [Project Page](https://ty0402.github.io/MMEditing/) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20339)

### UniFlow-Audio: Unified Flow Matching for Audio Generation from Omni-Modalities (arXiv 2025)

Xuenan Xu, Jiahao Mei, Zihao Zheng, **Ye Tao**, Zeyu Xie, Yaoyun Zhang, Haohe Liu, Yuning Wu, Ming Yan, Wen Wu, Chao Zhang, Mengyue Wu.

- Introduces a unified flow-matching framework for audio generation across both time-aligned and non-time-aligned tasks.
- Supports omni-modal conditioning (text, audio, and video) with strong parameter efficiency and competitive quality across multiple generation tasks.
- [Project Page](https://wsntxxn.github.io/uniflow_audio/) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24391)
